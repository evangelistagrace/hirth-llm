Comparison diagram of two-stroke scavenging systems with cross-sections through intake slots.
Shows three variants: MAN-Umkehrspülung (MAN reverse scavenging),
MAN-Umkehrspülung mit Nachladescheibe (with boost disc),
and Schnürle-Umkehrspülung (Schnürle loop scavenging).
A = Auslaß (exhaust), E = Einlaß (intake). Used for optimizing gas exchange in two-stroke engines.
Keywords: Spülverfahren, Umkehrspülung, Schnürle, Ladungswechsel, Einlaßschlitze.
